Rise and Shine

Gordon Freeman is a theoretical physicist and the main protagonist of the Half-Life series. He is known for his silent demeanor, his iconic crowbar, and his role in the resistance against the alien Combine forces. Freeman's journey begins at the Black Mesa Research Facility, where an experiment goes awry, leading to a catastrophic resonance cascade that opens a portal to another dimension.

Companions

Gordon Freeman has several companions throughout the Half-Life series, including Barney Calhoun, who serves as his guide and friend in the early stages of the game. Later on in Half-Life 2, he meets Alyx Vance, the daughter of his former coworker, Eli Vance.
